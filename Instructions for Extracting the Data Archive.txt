Since the data text files in the archive are policy documents in txt format with Chinese names, to avoid Chinese character corruption after extraction, please download the "School sports(central and local)data.zip" archive directly, extract it on your local computer (Windows or macOS), and then run the Python code. Do not extract this archive directly on a Linux server. The original text files in the archive have Chinese document names, and extracting them in a Linux environment may result in corrupted file names.
